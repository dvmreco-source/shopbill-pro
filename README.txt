SHOPBILL PRO V3 - Barcode labels edition

NEW IN V3
- Automatic unique internal product barcode generation (SBP00000001, SBP00000002, ...).
- CODE128 barcode rendering built into the app (no external barcode service required).
- Print a label for one product or print labels for the entire catalogue.
- Each label includes product name, selling price, CODE128 barcode and readable barcode number.
- Label print layout targets 58mm paper width.

EXISTING FEATURES
- Product catalogue, barcode/SKU, selling price and optional stock.
- Camera barcode scanning where supported; manual entry and scanner-as-keyboard also work.
- Billing, discounts, customer details, invoice numbering, saved bills, daily reports, CSV export, PDF printing, backup/restore.
- Bluetooth ESC/POS connection attempt for compatible BLE printers only.

ANDROID INSTALLATION
1. Extract this ZIP on your phone (Files app > Downloads > ZIP > Extract).
2. Upload the extracted folder to an HTTPS static website host. This package is website/PWA source, not a signed APK.
3. Open the HTTPS website address in Android Chrome.
4. Tap Chrome menu (three dots) > Install app or Add to Home screen.
5. Open ShopBill Pro > Products. Enter product name and price. Leave Barcode/SKU empty and save; the app creates a unique SBP barcode automatically. Or tap Generate before saving.
6. Tap Label next to a product, or Print all barcode labels. In Android print preview choose your thermal printer and paper width 58mm, then print.
7. Use scanner or camera from New Bill to scan the printed barcode.

IMPORTANT PRINTING NOTES
- This label page is designed for 58mm-wide print media. The actual label height and feed/cut behaviour depend on the printer driver.
- A normal 58mm receipt printer usually prints on continuous thermal paper, not adhesive sticker stock. To make sticky product labels, use compatible 58mm adhesive thermal label roll and a printer that supports it, or print on sticker sheets with a suitable label printer.
- Browser printing is the label-printing method in this release. The existing direct Bluetooth ESC/POS feature is separate and works only with compatible BLE ESC/POS printers. Many inexpensive printers use Classic Bluetooth SPP and will not connect directly from Chrome.
- CODE128 values generated by ShopBill Pro are internal shop inventory identifiers, not GS1/official manufacturer retail barcodes. Do not use them as official product GTINs for external retail marketplaces.
- App records are stored locally in this browser/device. Export backups regularly. No cloud sync. Not certified tax/accounting software.
